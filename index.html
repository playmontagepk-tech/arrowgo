A quiet arrow puzzle

Find the free path.

Every arrow points somewhere. If the way ahead is open, it slides off the board. Tap them in the right order, open the next path, and empty the maze one move at a time.

How to play Privacy policy

Single player. No timer. No account. Plays offline on iPhone and Android.

Three lives. No clock.

An arrow can leave only when the cells in front of it are clear. A blocked tap costs a life. Restart any time and try a different order.

01

Read the grid

Arrows point up, down, left, or right. The ones with an open path are ready to go.

02

Tap the free one

Clearing an arrow often opens the next. Hints and path guides are there if a board gets tight.

03

Empty the board

Easy grids grow into larger mazes, including hard and silhouette puzzles. Pinch to zoom on big boards.

Made for a quiet break.

Focus, not speed. Study the board and enjoy the moment an arrow clears.

Campaign puzzles

Easy, hard, and silhouette levels that get larger as you go.

Help when you want it

Path guides, hints, and a restart. Three lives on every level.

Your own volume

Music, sound effects, and vibration can be turned down or off. Progress stays on your device.
